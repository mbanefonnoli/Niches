# Niche: HoReCa equipment and supplies

Use the `studio-core` skill for the workflow and `niche-horeca` for market knowledge.

- Prospects: `prospects.csv` (target 20, best 10 get samples).
- Learnings across clients: `notes.md`.
- Clients: `clients/<client>/` — create with `python scripts/new-client.py horeca "<Client Name>"` from the repo root.

Default ad language: Romanian. Every technical figure comes from the manufacturer datasheet in the client's `assets/`.
