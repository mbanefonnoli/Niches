## Lessons from past jobs

- 2026-11-10 winter hoodie: AI image edit (Nano Banana) changed the printed typeface and verse layout on the hoodie. Crossfading the AI scene into the real product shot made the mismatch obvious. Always compare printed text and typeface with the real product photo before using any AI-edited product image.
- Real person in a birthday photo had her full name on a sign behind her; blurred before use. Check backgrounds for names and private details.
- A real photo of someone wearing the product beats an AI edit; ask the shop for one first.
