## Lessons from past jobs

- 2026-11-10 winter hoodie: AI image edit (Nano Banana) changed the printed typeface and verse layout on the hoodie. Crossfading the AI scene into the real product shot made the mismatch obvious. Always compare printed text and typeface with the real product photo before using any AI-edited product image.
- Real person in a birthday photo had her full name on a sign behind her; blurred before use. Check backgrounds for names and private details.
- A real photo of someone wearing the product beats an AI edit; ask the shop for one first.
- Live Design canvas with the static ads: https://claude.ai/artifact/VSe4kvSqtxueyDWGNVjcD5 (4:5 with model, 1:1 product, 1:1 with model).
- 2026-11-22 "but God." hoodie: Kling image-o1 (1k, image_to_image, real hoodie photo as 图片1, prompt told it to keep lettering exact) reproduced the print layout closely, far better than the Nano Banana edit. Still check small text letter by letter against the real photo. Uploaded via kling.ai/api/mcp/files; needs kling.ai and s15-kling.klingai.com allowed.
- Models in ads are AI-generated people: disclose as AI-generated. Turn off the "AI Generated" corner label in Kling web settings only if disclosure is handled elsewhere in the ad.
