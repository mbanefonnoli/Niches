# Local retail and gift shops

## Who buys

Independent shops and cafés with a retail or gift corner, mostly in Constanța to start. Decision maker: the owner. Budgets are small and seasonal; they buy around moments (winter holidays, Easter, summer tourist season, Valentine's, 1 Martie, back to school).

Treat this niche honestly: it is a fast portfolio and testing ground, close to home, not the main revenue line. Keep packages simple and cheap to produce, and use the work to sharpen the workflow and build case studies for the higher-value niches.

Language: Romanian first (correct diacritics), with optional English for tourist-season or expat audiences. Tone: warm, personal, local.

## What they lack (typical `weakness_noted`)

- Phone photos posted without copy or offer.
- No ads at all, or boosted posts with no clear call to action.
- No seasonal plan: posting starts after the season has begun.

## Offer

| Package | Contents | Starting price to test (validate, then adjust) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Season pack | 4 static ads (4:5, 1:1) + 1 motion video 9:16 + captions, for one product or theme | €100–250 |
| Monthly local | 2 themes a month, statics + 1 video each | €200–400 / month |

## Angle bank (verify against the shop's real customers)

- Gift for someone dear; last-minute gift close by.
- Season mood: warm, cosy, winter, holidays.
- Local and personal: "found only here", "visit us" — only if true.
- Meaning behind the product (message, faith, place) — respectful, never mocking or exploiting beliefs.
- Bundle with the café: coffee + gift.

## Research sources

The shop's own Facebook/Instagram posts and comments, Google Maps reviews, what nearby shops are advertising (Meta Ad Library filtered by location terms), local Facebook groups for the city.

## Prospect sources

Google Maps for gift shops, concept stores, cafés with retail corners and souvenir shops in Constanța and Mamaia; local Facebook pages; walk-ins (local is the advantage).

## Deliverables and shot list

The real product on a person or in the shop (best); product flat-lay or hanger shot against a clean wall; in-shop context (counter, shelf, café table); end card with the shop name, area and call to action; seasonal decoration only if the shop actually has it.

## QA additions (on top of studio-core stage 6)

- Product print, colour and text exactly as the real item (AI edits changed a hoodie's print typeface in the first Lighthouse job — always compare with the real product photo).
- Price, discount and stock wording only as the owner approved; otherwise leave the `[PREȚ] lei` placeholder and flag it.
- Every person shown: owner-confirmed written consent; private details in the background (names, birthday signs, documents) removed or blurred.
- Address and opening hours match the shop's Google listing.
- Religious or cultural text quoted exactly as on the product, with correct reference.

## Outreach message (Romanian draft; adapt per shop)

> Bună, [Nume]! Am văzut că aveți [produs] în magazin — arată foarte bine, dar pe Facebook apare doar în poze simple. Am făcut 2 exemple de reclame pentru sezonul de iarnă (atașat, marcate „SAMPLE”). Dacă vă plac, pot pregăti un pachet complet pentru sezon la un preț fix. — [Semnătură]
