# HoReCa equipment and supplies

## Who buys

Distributors, importers and e-shops selling commercial kitchen and bar equipment and supplies to restaurants, cafés, hotels and caterers. Decision maker: owner, sales manager or the one-person marketing role. Smaller Romanian distributors usually have no in-house designer or video.

Language: Romanian for the Romanian market (correct diacritics), English for international distributors. Tone: practical, professional, no hype. Their buyers are chefs and owners who distrust marketing fluff.

## What they lack (typical `weakness_noted`)

- Product pages built from the manufacturer's spec PDF and white-background photos.
- No video of the machine working; no demonstration of capacity, speed or cleaning.
- Facebook/Instagram ads that are just a product photo with a price.
- Same creative across hundreds of SKUs.

## Angle bank (verify each against real buyer language in research)

- Speed in service: output per hour, cups per hour, cycle time — **only from the datasheet**.
- Running cost: energy, water, detergent per cycle — only with a source.
- Reliability and service: warranty length, local service, spare parts availability.
- Space: footprint for small kitchens and bars.
- Hygiene and cleaning time — describe features, never "certifies HACCP compliance" (equipment does not make a business HACCP compliant).
- Upgrade moment: new opening, season start (summer terrace, winter events), replacing a failed unit fast.

## Research sources

- The distributor's own site and their eMAG / marketplace listings and reviews.
- Manufacturer datasheets (the source of truth for every number).
- Romanian HoReCa forums and Facebook groups for owners and chefs; Google Maps reviews of cafés mentioning equipment.
- Competitor distributors' active ads in the Meta Ad Library.

## Prospect sources

Europages (filter: Romania, catering equipment), Romanian HoReCa trade fair exhibitor lists, marketplace seller pages on eMAG for "echipamente HoReCa", and warm introductions through existing HoReCa contacts (the affiliate blog partner network).

## Deliverables and shot list

Product hero on a real counter or kitchen context; close-ups of controls and build quality; in-use sequence (load → run → result); size reference next to a person or common object; a price/offer card the client fills in.

## QA additions (on top of studio-core stage 6)

- Every capacity, power (kW, V, phase), dimension and energy figure matches the datasheet exactly; units shown correctly (kW not KW, 230 V / 400 V).
- Machine model name and number match the SKU being sold; no other brand's logo visible.
- Generated kitchen scenes show plausible, safe use (no food next to open electrical panels, staff with hair tied, etc.).
- No hygiene, certification or energy-label claim without a document in `assets/`.
